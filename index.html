GOOD QUESTIONS

Things you might
want to know.

How does a project start?+

Start by telling us about your business, goals, and the kind of website you have in mind. We can then discuss the scope, priorities, and next steps.

How long does a website take?+

Timing depends on the size of the website, its features, content readiness, and feedback. We'll discuss a realistic timeline once the project requirements are clearer.

Will my website work on phones and tablets?+

Responsive design is part of our approach. Layouts should adapt to different screen sizes, and the finished site should be checked on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Can you redesign my existing website?+

Yes, you can ask us about a redesign. We'll need to understand your current site, what is working, and what you'd like to improve.

Can I request changes?+

Absolutely. The revision process and its scope should be agreed at the planning stage so everyone understands how feedback and changes will be handled.

Do you offer support after launch?+

Post-launch support can be discussed as part of your project. The available arrangements will depend on the needs and scope we agree on.
